# Privacy Policy for Ratatouille

**Last Updated: November 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Ratatouille** meal planning and recipe application ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web application and associated services.

[&larr; Back to Ratatouille Home](./)

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Ratatouille is designed to help you plan meals, discover recipes, and build shopping lists. When you use the Service, we process the following information:

- **Google Account Information:** When you sign in using Google OAuth2, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google. Your email address is used to identify your account and keep your conversations and preferences separate from other users.
- **Conversations & Meal Plans:** Messages you send to the assistant, along with generated menus, recipes, and shopping lists, are stored so you can view, search, and resume past conversations.
- **Preferences & Context:** Your saved settings—such as your selected cookbook preferences, interface language, and optional location or season context—are stored to personalize your meal suggestions.
- **Uploaded Ingredient Photos:** If you attach or capture a photo of ingredients, the image is processed to identify ingredients and suggest matching recipes.
- **Session Cookies:** We use a signed session cookie to keep you logged in and protect authentication requests.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve the Service for you:

- To authenticate your account and maintain your active session.
- To generate personalized meal plans, recipes, nutritional balance suggestions, and shopping lists.
- To remember your cookbook selections, location/season context, and conversation history across sessions.
- To deliver recipes or menus to external messaging channels (such as WhatsApp) when you explicitly ask the assistant to send them.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- **Storage:** Conversations and user preferences are stored in the application's configured database (such as SQLite or Google Cloud Firestore) and isolated per user account.
- **Authentication:** We use industry-standard Google OAuth2 for authentication. We never see or store your Google password.
- **Cookies:** Session state is maintained via a cryptographically signed HTTP session cookie.

## 4. Third-Party Services

To provide its core features, Ratatouille relies on third-party services:

- **Google OAuth2 & Google Cloud:** Used for sign-in and, when deployed in the cloud, application hosting and storage.
- **Google Gemini AI:** Your prompts, relevant conversation context, and any uploaded ingredient photos are sent to Google Gemini models to generate responses and detect ingredients.
- **WhatsApp (Optional):** When you explicitly request to share a menu or recipe via WhatsApp, the message content is transmitted through the configured WhatsApp integration.

Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how Google handles data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Your Choices and Data Deletion

- **Manage Conversations:** You can delete individual conversations or clear all of your conversations directly from the conversation history menu in the app.
- **Update Preferences:** You can change or clear your cookbook selections and location/season context at any time in the app settings.
- **Revoke Access:** You can revoke Ratatouille's access to your Google account at any time through your [Google Account Security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
